# Healthcare Workflow Guide — Documenting a Regulated Process

This sample shows how to document a workflow where every step must be traceable: patient referral intake in a clinic system.

## Workflow overview
1. Referral received (fax, portal, or phone).
2. Staff verify patient identity with two identifiers.
3. Insurance eligibility is checked and recorded.
4. Referral is routed to the correct department queue.
5. Patient is contacted to schedule within two business days.

## Documentation rules for regulated workflows
- Name the **actor** for every step (role, not a person's name).
- Record **what evidence is captured** at each step (for example, eligibility screenshot ID).
- State the **time limit** where one exists ("within two business days").
- Flag every step where protected health information (PHI) is viewed or transmitted.

## Verify the documentation
Walk the process with a staff member who did not write it. Any step they cannot complete from the document alone is a documentation bug — fix the document, not the reader.
